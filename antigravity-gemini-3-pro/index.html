• Tip 1

    Communicate your passion

    A bright face says that you are happy and comfortable, you know that mentees will behave, and any non-compliance will be addressed with the minimum of fuss. This becomes a positive self-fulfilling prophecy.

  • Tip 2

    Be Prepared

    Preparation is key to a successful session. Ensure you have all materials ready and a clear plan of action. This builds confidence and trust with your mentees.

  • Tip 3

    Listen Actively

    Active listening involves fully concentrating, understanding, responding, and then remembering what is being said. It is the foundation of effective communication.

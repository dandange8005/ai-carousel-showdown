Tip 1

Communicate your passion

A bright face says that you are happy and comfortable; mentees will behave, and any non‑compliance will be addressed with the minimum of fuss. This becomes a positive self‑fulfilling prophecy.

Tip 2

Set clear expectations

Explain what good looks like and how progress will be measured. Clarity removes friction and builds confidence.

Tip 3

Give timely feedback

Short feedback loops keep momentum and allow small course corrections before issues expand.

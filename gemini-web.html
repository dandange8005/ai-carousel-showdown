Tip 1

Communicate your passion

A bright face says that you are happy and comfortable, you know that mentees will behave, and any non-compliance will be addressed with the minimum of fuss. This becomes a positive self-fulfilling prophecy.

Set clear expectations

Define the process roadmap early. When participants know what is expected at each stage, anxiety decreases and engagement improves.

Listen actively

True communication is two-way. Pay attention not just to their words, but their tone and body language to understand their real needs.
